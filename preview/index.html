About Me

Arezoo Yousefi

Jewelry Designer

I specialize in jewelry design, concept development, and visual presentation. My services include original jewelry concepts, bespoke design, collection development, and digital renderings tailored to each project’s creative direction and requirements.

I collaborate with jewelry brands, manufacturers, and industry professionals on individual designs, exclusive collections, and creative development projects.

The Design Process

From a first conversation to the final design

  1. Initial discussion

    The process begins with an initial discussion about your ideas, preferences, and project requirements.

  2. Inspiration and references

    Reference images, sketches, gemstones, architectural forms, cultural influences, or personal stories can all serve as inspiration.

  3. Design concept

    From there, I develop the design concept and refine the details through a collaborative process.

  4. Digital renderings

    Digital renderings communicate the design’s form, proportions, materials, and gemstones through detailed visual presentations.

  5. Final design

    The final design aligns with the agreed creative direction.
